layers and six cross-cutting package families (§II.C of the paper).

The table below maps each package to its CoSMA layer, folder in the repository,
generation method (Jinja2 or static), and whether it appears in Fig. 4 of the
paper (non-100% error-free rate in at least one configuration).

## Fig. 4 packages (non-100% error-free in ≥ 1 configuration)

Seven packages exhibited non-100% SLOC error-free rates in at least one
backbone × snapshot combination (Fig. 4 of the paper):

| Package | Why it is structurally challenging |
|---------|-----------------------------------|
| `MissionExecutionPackage` | Time-slice definitions with occurrence/usage quantity operators |
| `TechnicalPortsPackage` | Explicit port direction typing and conjugation |
| `FunctionSpecificationPackage` | Cross-layer `satisfy` links to Requirements |
| `SystemSpecificationPackage` | ISQ type constraints and CoSMA quantity bindings |
| `FunctionalRequirementsPackage` | Large `requirement def` bodies with nested constraints |
| `TechnicalRequirementsPackage` | Dense `refine`/`satisfy` traceability matrix |
| `ContextPackage` | Stochastic operational parameters (sector, entry point, archetype) |

The remaining 20 packages reached 100.0% SLOC error-free in both backbones
and both snapshots and are omitted from Fig. 4 for clarity.
